FAQ

Asked, answered.

Is it really free and open source?

Yes — MIT licensed. Source, issues, and releases all live on GitHub. Fork it, hack it, ship it.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows, as installer and portable builds. The app is Electron with pure HTML/CSS/JS renderers.

Where do my clips live?

On your disk only: %APPDATA%\ClipTap\clips\. Nothing ever leaves your machine.

Any telemetry or accounts?

None. Zero network requests, zero accounts. Pause monitoring anytime from the tray icon.

Something broke — how do I report it?

Open %APPDATA%\ClipTap\ClipTap.log (or tray → Open log file) and attach the last lines to a GitHub issue.
